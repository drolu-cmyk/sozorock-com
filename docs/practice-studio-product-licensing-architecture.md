# Practice Studio Product and Licensing Architecture

Status: Approved strategic direction
Owner: SozoRock Tech Inc.

## Principle
Practice Studio is a technology product in its own right. Education is one application of the product, not its category.

## Entity roles
### SozoRock Tech Inc.
- owns and develops Practice Studio;
- operates the core product platform;
- manages product IP, architecture, integrations, security, reliability, vendor relationships, and product roadmap;
- may license or provide the product to authorized organizations.

### SozoRock School
- is an initial customer/operator of a Practice Studio deployment;
- configures the platform for its professional-development pathways;
- remains responsible for School-specific enrollment, program administration, participant policies, and any credentials it is permitted to issue;
- does not define the core product category.

## Tenant model
The core platform must be multi-tenant ready even if the first release operates a single tenant.

Tenant configuration should include:
- tenant identity and branding;
- simulated enterprise configuration;
- authorized roles/pathways;
- participant population;
- integrations;
- data retention;
- assessment configuration;
- human assessor roles;
- model/provider policy;
- security policy;
- billing/licensing metadata.

## Product surfaces
1. Public Practice Studio product surface under SozoRock Technology.
2. Authenticated Practice Studio application.
3. Tenant-specific workplace experience.
4. Administrative/operator console.
5. Assessor/reviewer console.
6. Integration and provisioning services.

School marketing may link to Practice Studio, but the core product should not visually or technically depend on School branding.

## Initial licensing posture
Before formal legal drafting, model School as an internal licensee/operator rather than collapsing ownership.

Possible later commercial models to evaluate:
- per active participant/employee;
- per active simulated workplace;
- enterprise annual license;
- platform + usage;
- managed deployment;
- private/dedicated environment;
- assessment/evidence add-on;
- integration/professional services separate from license.

Do not publish pricing until unit economics and customer discovery support it.

## Product-category positioning
Primary:
Persistent enterprise work simulation platform.

Possible enterprise language:
- workforce simulation
- role-readiness simulation
- enterprise experience platform
- simulated workplace infrastructure

Avoid positioning the core product as:
- LMS
- online school
- course platform
- quiz platform
- virtual classroom
- generic AI tutor

## Eligibility and credits
When applying to startup/product programs, represent the facts accurately:
- applicant: SozoRock Tech Inc. where eligible;
- product: Practice Studio;
- relationship: SozoRock School is an initial deployment/license use case;
- never disguise consulting/services revenue or nonprofit activity as product revenue;
- never claim external customers, revenue, funding, licenses, or production scale that do not exist.

This structure may improve fit for technology-product programs, but each provider's current eligibility terms govern and must be checked at application time.
