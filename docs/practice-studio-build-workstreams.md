# Practice Studio Build Workstreams

## 1. Product Office
Owns product constitution, scope, decision log, roadmap, vocabulary, release gates.

## 2. Enterprise World
Creates the simulated company's identity, organization chart, teams, locations, clients, vendors, policies, systems, histories, relationships, and normal operating rhythm.

## 3. Experience Design
Designs employee entry, workday shell, messages, meetings, people, files, systems, support, required training, one-pagers, debriefs, and accessibility.

## 4. Platform Engineering
Builds identity, world state, event orchestration, persistence, APIs, realtime state, consequence logic, and integration framework.

## 5. Real Systems
Builds controlled integrations for AWS, GitHub, email/calendar, IAM, cybersecurity, data, and AI tooling.

## 6. AI and Voice
Builds simulated-person behavior, grounding, memory, tool use, realtime speech, transcripts, interruption handling, and provider routing.

## 7. Evidence and Assessment
Builds evidence ledger, artifact capture, competency mapping, assessor console, debrief grounding, integrity and replay.

## 8. Security and Trust
Threat model, isolation, permissions, secrets, abuse prevention, safe failure, cost containment, auditability, privacy, retention.

## 9. Practice Design
Creates authentic ordinary work, required training, one-pagers, engagements, incidents, artifacts, and pathway-specific responsibilities.

## 10. QA and Red Team
Tests realism, continuity, accessibility, security, cost, model failure, external-tool failure, state corruption, and learner attempts to exploit the simulation.

## First release gate
Do not call the product proved until one employee can complete several believable workdays with:
- persistent identity;
- ordinary work;
- real systems;
- voice/text interactions;
- evolving situation;
- meaningful choices;
- consequences;
- professional deliverable;
- grounded debrief;
- evidence record.
