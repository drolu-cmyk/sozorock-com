# Practice Studio Product Ownership and Licensing Model

Status: Product strategy approved
Product owner entity: SozoRock Tech Inc.

## Position
Practice Studio is a standalone technology product of SozoRock Technology. It is not an education product owned by SozoRock School.

SozoRock School is an initial operating customer/licensee of Practice Studio and uses the product to deliver its professional pathways.

## Entity relationship
SozoRock Tech Inc.:
- owns Practice Studio product IP, software, architecture, platform services, product roadmap, integrations, and commercial licensing;
- develops and operates the Practice Studio Engine;
- contracts with infrastructure and technology vendors where appropriate;
- may license Practice Studio to SozoRock School and, later, external organizations.

SozoRock School:
- licenses/uses Practice Studio for its programs;
- defines pathway-specific practice requirements, participant operations, assessors, and program rules;
- does not own the underlying platform merely because it is the first major implementation.

## Public positioning
Practice Studio should be described as a technology product:
- persistent enterprise work simulation platform;
- enterprise experience engine for simulated employment;
- controlled real-tool work environment;
- evidence-based capability platform.

Avoid positioning it primarily as:
- an LMS;
- e-learning software;
- a course platform;
- a school portal;
- a gamified learning app.

## Commercial model
Potential future licensing models:
- internal license to SozoRock School;
- per-active-user enterprise license;
- annual platform license;
- usage-based infrastructure pass-through;
- managed implementation and integration fees;
- employer-specific simulation packages;
- assessment/evidence modules;
- private enterprise environments.

Commercial terms are not fixed by this document.

## Product customers
Potential customers include:
- professional education providers;
- employers;
- consulting and professional-services firms;
- workforce development organizations;
- cybersecurity teams;
- AI/data/cloud teams;
- enterprises running onboarding, readiness, incident, governance, or role simulations.

## Funding/partner representation
When applying for technology-product startup programs, Practice Studio may be presented as a SozoRock Tech Inc. software product if all application statements remain accurate.

Do not represent School enrollment revenue or consulting revenue as Practice Studio software revenue unless it is actually recognized under a documented licensing/product arrangement.

Do not use nonprofit status or Foundation eligibility for Technology benefits unless a program expressly allows it.

## Internal licensing
Before material commercial scale, document at minimum:
- product ownership;
- School license scope;
- data controller/processor roles;
- infrastructure responsibilities;
- support responsibilities;
- permitted use;
- branding;
- privacy/security responsibilities;
- pricing or intercompany accounting treatment where required.

Formal legal and tax terms should be reviewed by qualified counsel/accounting professionals before reliance.
