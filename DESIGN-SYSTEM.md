# US Open School design
The current implementation is the approved architectural Open School, not the historical laptop, stone or document concepts in QA files.

Use Plus Jakarta Sans for headings and Source Sans 3 for body text. The authoritative tokens and responsive rules live in `src/open-school.css`; do not maintain a second token table here. Preserve ink/steel and cobalt, generous editorial spacing, clear buttons and four distinct disciplines.

The hero is conceptual architecture with four entrances, not physical premises or actual learners. Optional motion is a Blender 2.5D projection of the approved artwork; see [source and rendering](artwork/blender/README.md). Preserve pause, reduced motion, mobile framing and the still fallback. Never describe a still or CSS pan as a Blender render.

Keep the real director portrait in teaching/about content, with one visible homepage attribution. Never fabricate testimonials, cohorts or outcomes. Avoid repeated section illustrations, fake dashboards, emoji icons, purple gradients and decorative connector lines.

Source content must remain readable without JavaScript. Review contrast, typography, keyboard focus, navigation and responsive screenshots for the changed experience. Current verification belongs in the change's CI evidence; historical QA images are not evidence for a new revision.
