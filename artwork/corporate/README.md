# Corporate visual and source notes

The corporate system is separate from the preserved School system. Use Instrument Sans (self-hosted, OFL), white, Basalt #111315, Mist #F3F4F5, Ash #687078 and Mineral #A63F2B. CSS tokens live in public/corporate.css. Headings reach 112px on desktop and 48–56px on mobile; body copy is 18–22px, and corporate footer links/legal copy are 16px. Preserve flat editorial sections and visible keyboard focus.

The structural joint is a conceptual OpenAI-generated illustration, not a real product, physical test or Blender render. Original pixels are retained in structural-joint-source.png. The direction was a close architectural material study of two fitted basalt blocks, one horizontal and one upright, with an exposed interlocking joint and neutral light on a white background. No text, people, logos or implied measured results. The website intentionally uses a static poster. Optional motion was not required, and no unused video-generation dependency was added.

Optimized exports: joint-desktop-v1.webp (1536x1024 source frame) and joint-mobile-v1.webp, with sizes recorded in release QA. Desktop and mobile use separate responsive sources. Corporate social images are 1200x630 PNG; School retains its distinct sculpture artwork. Interface images are real public Foundation captures with source/date/limits recorded in asset-provenance.json. Rights in Foundation interfaces remain with the Foundation.

The corporate site reuses the existing S mark; it does not introduce a new symbol. Existing School Blender artwork and scripts remain under artwork/blender and are not represented as new corporate animation.
