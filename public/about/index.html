Our business

We develop technology products, engineer business systems and build practical skills. CB-CAP is our flagship spatial-intelligence product for care access. SozoRockSchool develops practical AI and cybersecurity capability through virtual programs and human assessment.

The technology business is separate from The SozoRock Foundation and from SozoRock Tech Inc., Canada. This website represents SozoRock Tech Inc., New York.

How we work

Define the operating problem

Identify the people affected, the decisions involved and the constraints a solution must respect. Agree on a useful outcome before choosing the technology.

Test the approach early

Use clear requirements, visible prototypes and explicit data assumptions. Evaluate the experience with the people who will use and operate it.

Build for operation

Connect product delivery to access controls, testing, release discipline and ongoing ownership. Plan how the system will be supported and improved after launch.
