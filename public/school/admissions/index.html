Know the commitment before you apply.

12 weeks · 100% virtual · 3 to 6 hours/week · United States

USD $299 introductory total per program: $49 enrollment + $250 tuition.

One clear introductory price.

USD $49 enrollment + USD $250 tuition = USD $299 total for one twelve-week Nano-Credential. This introductory price applies to the first US intake in each of the four programs. It includes guided practice, the applied project, human assessment and credential issuance when requirements are met. There is no recurring subscription or separate credential-issuance fee.

The USD $299 total is the amount paid to SozoRockSchool. A computer and reliable internet are required; equipment is not included. Any optional external certification or tool purchase must be clearly identified before enrollment.

There is no application payment.

The USD $49 enrollment fee is payable only after you accept an offer; the remaining USD $250 tuition is payable before the program starts. No payment is due with an application. The offer will state payment dates and cancellation and refund terms before you commit. Do not send payment or card details through the contact form.

Before you receive an offer

Review the program focus, weekly commitment and credential scope. Intake dates, entry expectations, equipment requirements and applicable fee and cancellation terms must be provided before you make a commitment.

What happens next

  1. Choose the program that fits your goals.
  2. Follow the application route shown on the application page.
  3. Read the offer and its terms. Accept only if it is right for you.
  4. Review the published tuition, enrollment fee and refund terms before accepting an offer or making any payment.
