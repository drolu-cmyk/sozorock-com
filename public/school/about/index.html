Experience behind the programs

Dr. Adeyemo designs applied assignments, supervises experiential projects and assesses master’s-level cybersecurity capstones and undergraduate technology projects. His work connects business, technology, strategy and practical learning.

He holds a Doctor of Business Administration in Strategic Management, with distinction, from Liberty University, and a Master of Business Administration from the University of Roehampton.

His teaching approach asks learners to test their work, explain their choices and respond to evidence.

One school. Four distinct responsibilities.

Applied AI Systems focuses on building and testing. Cybersecurity GRC focuses on risk and controls. Identity & Access Management focuses on permissions and authentication. AI Governance focuses on responsibility and review.
