Learning should be easier to reach.

We aim to make this website usable with keyboards, screen readers and mobile devices. We use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as a design and testing target; this is not a certification of complete conformance.

Controls that help

  • Skip links and visible keyboard focus.
  • Text labels, structured headings and SVG controls.
  • Still artwork and reduced-motion support.
  • Form labels and error messages that keep your entered information available for correction.

Tell us about a barrier

Describe the page, the task and the assistive technology or browser involved. You do not need to disclose a diagnosis. We can use that information to investigate the problem and discuss another way to access the content.
