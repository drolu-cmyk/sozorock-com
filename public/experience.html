Twelve weeks to build, test and explain.

You do not have to arrive with every answer. You do need to practice, explain your decisions and respond to feedback.

12 weeks · 100% virtual · 3–6 hours/week · United States

Week 1

Understand

Define the problem, its context and what a good response needs to do.

Week 4

Practice and explain

Complete guided exercises. Explain your choices and respond to feedback.

Week 8

Apply

Bring your skills together in a scoped project. Test the approach and address its limits.

Week 12

Present for review

Submit your evidence for human assessment. Address any required revisions.

A person reviews your submission.

An assessor evaluates your submission against the program requirements. You may be asked to revise your evidence. A separate authorized person confirms credential issuance after requirements are met. Attendance alone does not earn a credential.

Each issued credential has a unique verification ID.

Plan for consistent participation.

Set aside 3–6 hours each week. Delivery is 100% virtual. The intake schedule, equipment expectations and assessment requirements must be confirmed before an offer is issued.

If your circumstances change, use the support route provided during onboarding. Ask for help early so the teaching team can discuss what is possible.
