About the company

SozoRock Technology is the public corporate brand of SozoRock Tech Inc., New York. Our work connects software development, data presentation and the infrastructure needed to put systems into use.

Applied learning

SozoRockSchool is a learning activity operated by the corporation. Its virtual programs bring guided practice, applied projects and human assessment together.

Dr. Oluwabiyi Adeyemo serves as Director of Learning, AI & Cybersecurity. His School profile explains the learning approach and program leadership.

An independent Foundation

SozoRock Technology develops technology for selected initiatives of The SozoRock Foundation, an independent nonprofit organization. The Foundation is not a corporate subsidiary or division.

Its public benefit programs and evidence experiences remain on Foundation domains. Corporate work stories describe specific engineering contributions, with the scope and limitations made explicit.
