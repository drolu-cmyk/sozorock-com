Place Intelligence Engineering

Keep the source in view.

Geographic resolution and public evidence presentation in Place Intelligence, operated by the independent Foundation.

The problem

A location search can suggest more precision than the evidence supports. Geographic resolution must preserve the distinction between the place entered and the area measured.

Evidence presentation

Place Intelligence brings geographic discovery and public evidence into the same experience. The engineering concern is to carry the source, geography, date and limitations with the evidence rather than leave them behind after search.

Built by SozoRock Technology

SozoRock Technology designs the architecture and product experience, engineers the software and data systems, and builds the cloud infrastructure behind Place Intelligence. Geographic resolution and structured evidence contracts connect searches with supporting county evidence.

City and ZIP searches do not create city-level or ZIP-level measurements from county data. The Foundation leads the mission, nonprofit programs and implementation; SozoRock Technology builds the technology that supports them.

Observed result and limits

Public evidence supports exploration and questions for further review. Area estimates do not diagnose individuals, establish causation or replace local knowledge. Read the displayed source and measurement geography before using a result.

Place Intelligence is operated by The SozoRock Foundation. The corporate story has a distinct engineering purpose; the public evidence destination remains on its Foundation domain.
