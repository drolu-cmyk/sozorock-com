Funders and enterprises

Health systems and service organizations

DATA & SECURITY

Trust starts with
what the data means.

CB-CAP is designed around deidentified, place-based intelligence. Implementation requires agreed data permissions, access controls, source definitions and human review.

About this interactive demonstration

The map uses historical 2010 ZIP Code Tabulation Areas around New York's Capital Region. These statistical areas are not current USPS delivery boundaries.

Barrier scores, service locations, demand indices and scenario effects are synthetic. Coverage circles illustrate service reach; they are not road-network travel-time estimates. The demonstration contains no resident records and does not provide validated forecasts.
