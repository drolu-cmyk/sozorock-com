Who it is for

You want to understand how an AI system behaves beyond a demonstration. You may already use AI tools, support technology or want a more disciplined approach to building and testing.

What you will learn

  • Define a bounded problem and its intended use.
  • Build an AI-enabled system suited to the problem.
  • Evaluate results against stated expectations.
  • Identify failure conditions and human oversight needs.

The twelve weeks

Week 1

Understand

Define the problem, its context and what a good response needs to do.

Week 4

Practice and explain

Complete guided exercises. Explain your choices and respond to feedback.

Week 8

Apply

Bring your skills together in a scoped project. Test the approach and address its limits.

Week 12

Present for review

Submit your evidence for human assessment. Address any required revisions.

Your final submission

An AI system within the agreed scope, evaluation evidence and an explanation of its limits and oversight needs.

The precise brief and assessment criteria are provided for the intake. Website illustrations are not learner submissions.

A person reviews your submission.

An assessor evaluates your submission against the program requirements. You may be asked to revise your evidence. A separate authorized person confirms credential issuance after requirements are met. Attendance alone does not earn a credential.

Each issued credential has a unique verification ID.

Before you apply

Check the entry expectations and equipment requirements for the intake. These must be confirmed before offers are issued. Allow 3–6 hours each week and plan to participate consistently.

Who this is not for

This is not a substitute for a degree, professional license or third-party certification. It is not designed for someone seeking a credential through attendance alone.

What the credential covers

A SozoRock Nano-Credential recognizes successful completion of its program requirements. It is not a degree, diploma, academic credit, professional license or third-party certification. It does not guarantee employment.
