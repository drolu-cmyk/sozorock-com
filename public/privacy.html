Understand what this website collects.

Last updated: September 5, 2026.

Website access

SozoRock Tech Inc., New York, operates this US website. Hosting services process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website. This release does not add advertising trackers, analytics cookies or marketing subscriptions.

Enquiries

When the contact service is available, it requests your name, email address, enquiry category and message so we can address your enquiry. These fields are not used to enroll you or subscribe you to marketing. Records are stored in the United States and scheduled for deletion after 30 days. Automated expiration may take additional processing time.

Applications

Applications are currently closed. When activated, the US application form collects your name, email, program choice, learning goals and consent to review and respond to your application. Records are stored in the United States with access restricted to authorized staff. Records expire after 90 days; physical deletion is asynchronous, and recovery backups may retain earlier values within their recovery window. No payment is collected by this form.

Your choices

Contact fields remain in the page while it is open. They are not saved persistently in your browser. You can request access, correction or deletion through the privacy enquiry category. We may need to verify the request before disclosing or changing personal information.

Scope

This notice concerns www.sozorock.com and its apex redirect. Other websites have their own policies. The contact service displays its availability before collecting an enquiry.
