# Cross-Functional Enterprise Developments

## Model
A development is something that happens in Harborline. It is not a learner-facing scenario container.

The engine stores the authoritative facts. Employees receive different views according to:
- role;
- system access;
- messages and meetings they were included in;
- evidence they actually inspected;
- facts they previously discovered.

## Atlas Actions rollout
The initial shared development is ordinary launch-readiness work for a controlled Atlas Actions release.

Different functions see different starting information:
- IAM receives an identity/access signal;
- Applied AI Systems receives a restricted-retrieval evaluation result;
- GRC sees a launch-evidence concern;
- AI Governance sees a deployment-gate concern.

The hidden underlying state includes residual access, excessive service-identity access, document misclassification, a long-lived vendor token and unapproved public-AI use.

No employee receives that hidden list.

## Discovery
Facts become visible through authorized systems, evidence, communication or prior discoveries. Discovery is recorded as an event with source and evidence reference.

## Consequences
Cross-functional state is computed from facts discovered and actions actually completed. Launch conditions therefore evolve from employee work rather than from a scripted branching narrative.

## Decision
The engine may support proceed, conditional launch, limited release or delay. It must not force a single answer when several positions are professionally defensible. The employee must explain rationale, evidence, uncertainty, owners and remaining conditions.
