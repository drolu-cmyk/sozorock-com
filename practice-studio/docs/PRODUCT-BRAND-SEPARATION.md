# Product Brand Separation

## Decision
The standalone technology product must have its own commercial identity.

SozoRock School is a licensee/deployment of the product. It is not the product owner and the product is not an education-only system.

## Current naming state
Practice Studio remains the working internal/category name while commercial naming is resolved.

Do not hard-code Practice Studio as the final external brand into:
- tenant contracts;
- provider integrations;
- database identifiers;
- URLs that would be costly to migrate;
- customer-facing legal documents;
- external product claims.

## Product hierarchy
SozoRock Technology
-> standalone product brand [to be selected]
-> Practice Studio Engine [working internal engine name]
-> licensed tenant/deployment
-> simulated enterprise
-> employee role and experience

SozoRock School US and Canada are initial licensed deployments.

## Naming criteria
The final name should:
- work for employers as well as workforce/education deployments;
- sound credible in procurement and enterprise security review;
- avoid school/course/training language;
- avoid narrowing the product to one profession;
- be ownable enough for trademark/domain review;
- support a concise verbal identity.

## Clearance
Web collision checks are preliminary only. Before public adoption, perform formal trademark, corporate-name and domain clearance in intended markets.
