# Longitudinal Capability Evidence

## Purpose
Practice Studio should answer: What has this person demonstrated they can actually do?

The answer comes from work performed across the simulated employment period, not from a single score.

## Dimensions
The initial evidence model tracks:
- investigation;
- execution;
- evidence use;
- judgment;
- professional artifacts;
- communication;
- response to feedback;
- independence.

## Observation
A capability observation must point to a work context and may reference concrete evidence. It records observed behavior, who or what observed it, time and the level of support present.

## Independence
Independence is described as:
- guided;
- supported;
- independent.

The system may show a longitudinal trend only when supported by multiple observations. It must not translate these labels into a percentage.

## Feedback
Manager or assessor feedback can create later evidence when the employee responds through real work. Improvement after feedback is therefore visible rather than merely noted.

## Portfolio
A participant-facing evidence portfolio may contain selected demonstrated work, artifacts and feedback. It must state clearly that the work occurred inside a simulated enterprise and is not employment history.

## Assessment
High-stakes professional judgment can be reviewed by a human assessor. Automated systems may organize evidence and surface patterns, but should not fabricate professional validity or infer capability beyond the available evidence.
