# Employee Onboarding and Day 1

## Experience principle
The participant should feel that they are joining a workplace, not completing a setup wizard.

Provisioning occurs behind the scenes.

## Provisioned before arrival
- Practice Studio principal;
- simulated employee record;
- role, team and manager;
- mailbox;
- calendar;
- system entitlements;
- sandbox lease;
- required policies/training.

## First visible experience
Example:

Good morning, Olu.
Your workplace access is ready. Your first team check-in starts at 9:15.

The employee can open messages, view the calendar, complete required workplace training and begin ordinary work.

## Email
The first implementation should use an isolated internal mailbox namespace. External delivery remains disabled until a deliberate provider and domain policy is approved.

## Policies
Day 1 includes concise one-page workplace materials where appropriate:
- confidentiality;
- acceptable use;
- privileged access/least privilege for IAM roles.

These materials exist because the employer requires them, not because the product is presenting a course module.

## Readiness
A participant should not enter Day 1 until identity, mailbox, calendar, entitlement and sandbox checks all pass.
