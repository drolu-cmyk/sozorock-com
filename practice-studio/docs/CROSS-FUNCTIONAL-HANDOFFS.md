# Cross-Functional Handoffs and Decision Record

## Purpose
A Harborline development creates interdependent professional work, not four isolated pathway experiences.

## Handoffs
Employees can request evidence, clarification or action from another function.

Examples:
- IAM asks Applied AI Systems which document access Atlas production actually requires.
- Applied AI Systems asks IAM to verify svc-atlas-prod entitlements.
- GRC requests remediation evidence from IAM and evaluation evidence from AI Systems.
- AI Governance requests deployment monitoring commitments from engineering and Risk.

Each handoff stays attached to the same enterprise development while retaining the requesting employee's work context.

## Dependencies
A downstream matter can wait on a specific upstream action. When the action is completed, the engine moves that dependency from waiting to ready.

## Launch positions
Each role can record its own professional position:
- proceed;
- conditional launch;
- limited release;
- delay.

There is no global score and no automatically selected winner.

## Decision record
The final cross-functional record aggregates:
- each role's position;
- evidence references;
- unresolved conditions;
- uncertainty;
- ownership.

The designated organizational decision owner makes the final launch decision according to role authority. Practice Studio evaluates the quality of the employee's work and judgment, not whether they guessed a hidden preferred answer.
