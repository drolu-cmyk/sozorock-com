# Harborline Systems Group

Harborline Systems Group is the current fictional enterprise used by the standalone product.

It is configuration, not product identity.

## Profile
A mid-sized U.S. and Canadian business-services and technology company with roughly 1,150 employees, 230 contractors and about 40 privileged administrators.

Headquarters: Albany, New York.
Other operating locations: Toronto, Ontario and Austin, Texas.

## Why this company shape
The organization must plausibly support all four initial professional pathways in one workplace:
- Applied AI Systems;
- Cybersecurity IAM;
- Cybersecurity GRC;
- AI Governance.

It therefore operates cloud infrastructure, enterprise identity, software repositories, regulated/data-intensive client work, internal AI systems, vendors and cross-border teams.

## Deliberate imperfections
Harborline is neither dysfunctional nor unrealistically perfect.

Its environment includes legacy nested groups, imperfect contractor offboarding dependencies, older cloud exceptions, uneven AI inventory quality, selected long-lived third-party credentials and occasional lag between written policy and engineering practice.

These facts create possible work. They do not guarantee incidents.

## Core internal AI system
Atlas is Harborline's enterprise knowledge assistant. Atlas Actions adds constrained workflow actions and is in controlled rollout.

## Fiction boundary
Harborline must always remain clearly fictional outside the immersive employee experience. It must never be represented as a real employer, client or customer of SozoRock Technology.
