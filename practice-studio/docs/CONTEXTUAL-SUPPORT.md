# Contextual Support

## Purpose
Practice Studio must remain usable by people who are new to a domain without becoming a theory-first LMS.

Support lives beside the work.

## Supported forms
- plain-language definition;
- acronym expansion;
- example;
- policy excerpt;
- one-page guide;
- concise how-to.

## Rules
Support:
- appears because of the current work context or a term the employee encountered;
- is optional unless a workplace policy explicitly requires reading/training;
- does not reveal hidden assessment criteria;
- does not identify a secret "correct answer";
- does not prescribe approve/deny/escalate decisions;
- does not interrupt the work with quizzes;
- returns the employee to the same matter.

## Evidence
Opening support may be recorded for product analytics and reconstruction where appropriate, but using help is not automatically a negative assessment signal. Real professionals consult policies, documentation and colleagues.

## Example
An employee sees "least privilege" in an access request and opens a short definition. If more help is needed, a one-page access-review guide explains what to verify. The employee still has to inspect evidence and make the judgment.
