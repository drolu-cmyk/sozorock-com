# Employee Interaction Contract

## Principle
Practice Studio records work, not clicks.

Navigation, opening a page, expanding a definition, or reading a message may create operational telemetry, but these actions are not automatically competency evidence.

Evidence-worthy actions include:
- substantive workplace communication;
- joining and participating in required meetings;
- requesting relevant support;
- working in professional systems;
- access and governance decisions;
- submitting professional work;
- creating artifacts.

## Interaction surfaces
### Messages
Threaded workplace communication with realistic sender identity, timing, attachments, and reply behavior.

### Meetings
Voice and text are equivalent supported modalities. Captions are available. Meetings are grounded in calendar state and participant roles.

### Systems
Only systems authorized for the employee's role are visible. Launches return through an adapter boundary and are tied to the current work context.

### Support
Definitions, examples, policy excerpts, and one-page guidance are contextual. Support explains; it does not silently make the employee's decision.

### Work
Assignments and deliverables produce evidence only when the participant performs meaningful work. Merely opening an assignment is not evidence of capability.

## Evidence hygiene
The evidence ledger should not become surveillance-by-clickstream.

Product analytics and professional evidence are separate concerns. Product telemetry may help improve usability, while capability evidence must remain tied to work that is professionally meaningful.
