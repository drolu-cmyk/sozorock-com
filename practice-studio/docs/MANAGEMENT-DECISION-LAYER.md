# Management and Executive Decision Layer

## Principle
Managers and executives respond to employee work, evidence and unresolved conditions. They are not scripted narrators.

## Escalation
Employees should escalate when:
- unresolved conditions exceed their authority;
- risk acceptance is required;
- functions disagree materially;
- the launch decision belongs to a higher role.

## Manager review
Managers can:
- challenge evidence;
- ask follow-up questions;
- identify strengths and concerns;
- assign follow-up;
- require additional verification.

Manager review remains grounded in the employee's actual evidence trail.

## Executive authority
The final organizational decision belongs to the configured decision owner with explicit authority.

For the initial Harborline Atlas rollout, the CIO holds final launch authority, while the CISO, VP Data & AI, Risk Director and General Counsel can provide role-specific positions.

## Conditional approval
A conditional launch is not an abstract label. It creates explicit conditions with:
- owner;
- due date where relevant;
- required evidence;
- status.

Those conditions become real work items.

## No artificial consensus
Leadership may receive conflicting professional recommendations. The product does not collapse disagreement into a score. It records rationale, evidence, uncertainty and ownership.
